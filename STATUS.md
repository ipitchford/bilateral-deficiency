## Status meaning

“Candidate” describes the assurance state of the mathematics, not a journal
submission. Evidence Press, repository hosting, archival deposit, hashes, test
receipts, and public availability do not by themselves establish correctness,
novelty, independent reproduction, peer review, or community acceptance.

This package is an immutable child candidate of the parent TxGraffiti release.
It extends the mathematical scope without silently revising the historical
parent record.

## Frozen mathematical scope

The candidate introduces bilateral deficiency, proves its residual and
formula-graph interpretations, develops its algebra and width-sensitive
complexity, and uses it as a gap coordinate for regular graphs equipped with a
dominating induced matching. It also includes a connected cubic-DIM amplifier,
connected linear-gap families, and an order-50 lower bound and construction in
the cubic-DIM class.

The order statement is deliberately qualified:

> Order 50 is the minimum among finite simple cubic graphs admitting a
> dominating induced matching for which \(i(G)>\mu^*(G)\).

It is not a claim of minimum order among all cubic graphs.

## Open boundaries

- The exact cubic-DIM extremal constants remain open.
- The sign problem for proper simple exact signed-occurrence
  \((3,2,2)\)-CNF remains open.
- Classification of all order-50 cubic-DIM extremisers remains open.
- The minimum counterexample order outside the DIM class remains open.
- The universal theory is not fully formalised in a proof assistant.
- The literature search is bounded and does not establish global priority.
