## Non-vendored tools used in verification

- **Lean 4**, including `Std.Tactic.BVDecide.LRAT`, is used to check the direct
  LRAT certificates and finite terminal theorem. Lean is not vendored here;
  its upstream terms apply to installations used for replay.
- **CaDiCaL** was used to generate direct proof objects. It is not vendored and
  is not required to replay the packaged LRAT proofs.
- Apple clang, CPython, Git, and standard operating-system tools are environment
  dependencies, not redistributed components of this package.
- Pandoc and Google Chrome were used to render the release PDFs. They are not
  redistributed; their upstream terms apply to local installations.
